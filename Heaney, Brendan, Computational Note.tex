\begin{abstract}
In this note we provide the results of computational checks on the various
conjectures of Hong and Sommese's ``Number of Real Critical Points of
Cyclotomic Polynomials.'' We find a family of counterexamples to conjecture
3[3] and provide a strengthened form of conjecture 3[2] suggested by our numerical search.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

We place some examples below

First, we reintroduce some of their notation. For an odd squarefree integer
\(m>1\), the authors define \(N_m\) to be the number of zeroes of the
derivative of the mth cyclotomic polynomial. We define Drop to refer to a
decrease in the number of roots \(\Phi_m'\), Hong and Sommese conjectured
that, for an odd squarefree integer n and primes \(p,p',\ p<p',\ p,p'\nmid n\), the following statement
holds(Conjecture~3[3]):
\[
N_{np}\le N_{np'}
\]

However, in our numerical search, we found a large variety counterexamples to this conjecture. We provide some interesting examples below.

\section{An Observation}
Hong and Sommese propose the following conjecture.
\setcounter{theorem}{2}

\begin{conjecture}
\[
N_n=2k-1
\quad\text{if n is the product of k smallest odd primes}
\]
\end{conjecture}

We find support for two statements that, together, imply Hong and Sommesse's
Conjecture.

 We reintroduce further notation from the author's original paper. Let
\(m=p_1\cdots p_k\) be a product of \(k\) distinct odd primes.
Furthermore, let \(N_m^+\) and \(N_m^-\) for the numbers of positive and
negative real zeros of \(\Phi_m'\) (counted with multiplicity).

\paragraph{Conjecture}
For every such \(m\),
\[
N_m^+\ge k-1,
\qquad
N_m^-\ge k
\]
These conjectures both hold for the \(1{,}326{,}776\) tested cases and, together, imply conjecture 3[2].
